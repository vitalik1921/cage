# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First public version. `cage check`, `lock`, `review` (with `--record`) and `gate`; designs as `*.cage.mdx` documents next to the code, nothing generated on disk; adapters for `node:test` and Vitest; locks (`@final`, `@extendable`) with `--base` comparison; design coverage with `.cageignore`; recorded reviews as a second gate; a Stop-hook example for Claude Code.
